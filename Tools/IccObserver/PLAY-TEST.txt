ICC Navigator native preview - first integration test

1. Extract the artifact into your separate test-client folder, beside Data.
   Keep the supplied Interface/AddOns/ICCObserver folder structure.
2. Copy data/mmaps from your EXISTING ICC Navigator package into this folder.
   Data (game assets) and data/mmaps (navigation tiles) share a folder on Windows.
   The GitHub navigator repository does not contain the map binaries.
3. Launch WowICCObserver64.exe and enable ICC Observer in the AddOns list.
4. On your own test server, enter ICC and type /iccpreview on.
5. Target/pull Marrowgar manually. The preview collects 5 seconds of history.
   Blue centre lines: estimated Coldflame history, not verified damage bounds.
   Green line/yellow cross: proposed route/destination. A hold can show a cross.
   Drag the status panel to move it. /iccpreview off disables the preview.
   /console mobRangeCircles 0 hides the old distance circles.
6. If maps were missing, copy them, then toggle the preview off and on to retry.

No movement or spells are automated in this build. Fury combat comes later.
The preview reuses icc-navigator 0.8.2 encounter planning and bundled Detour.
Native CGUnit_C fields and aura functions replace external memory reads;
the original input DLL and helper are neither loaded nor packaged.
Planning runs on the render thread at most four times/second and has the
upstream 60ms planning budget; some frame-time impact is possible.
Only map 631 / NPC 36612 / Coldflame 36672 / Bone Storm aura 69076 are supported.
Custom server IDs or different flame duration require changing the profile.
The 5-second history and 4-yard avoidance margin are inherited estimates.
Depth-tested guides float 0.15 yards above sampled coordinates; they do not
conform to terrain. Missing observations hide routes; no claim of safety.

Test normal phase, Bone Storm, death, Bone Spike, zoning and toggling off.
If something is wrong, send Logs/ICCObserver.log and a screenshot of the panel.
The log records status changes (bounded at 10,000 lines per enable session).
Compilation and synthetic planner tests do not establish live playability.
